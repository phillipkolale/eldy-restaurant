About Eldy

Our story

Eldy opened with one idea: that Eldoret deserved a table as considered as anywhere else.

We cook Kenyan classics alongside kitchen staples, treating both with the same care — flame-grilled nyama choma next to a pan-seared catch of the day, mukimo plated with the same attention as a layered cake.

The dining room is warm and unhurried: an open kitchen, low light, linen on every table. We want you to stay for the second glass of wine.

Everything is sourced close to home. Produce and meat come in fresh each morning, and the menu shifts with what the season gives us.

Flame-grilledHand-platedLocally sourcedSeasonalUnhurried

Philosophy

Every plate should hit that elusive, cuisine-defining balance — sweet, salty, sour, and something worth savouring. We'd rather do fewer dishes well than a long menu of average ones.

Ambience

Soft light, an open kitchen you can watch service from, and tables built for lingering — Eldy is built for the evenings you don't want to rush.
